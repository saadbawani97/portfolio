Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information SB Studio collects through this website, why, who else handles it, and the choices you have. We've kept it short and plain, and we only collect what we need to reply to you.

In short: we don't use cookies, we don't sell or rent your information, and we only see what you choose to send us through our forms.

Who we are

SB Studio is a team of independent professionals based in Karachi, Pakistan, offering digital services to clients in Pakistan and abroad. When this policy says "we", "us" or "our", it means SB Studio. You can reach us at saad.bawanyy@gmail.com.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you use our project form, digital plan questions or join form, we receive what you enter, which can include:

  • your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address and business name;
  • the country you choose, which we use to show prices in your currency;
  • your answers to the digital plan questions (type of business, rough monthly revenue, what you already have online and your main goal), and the plan suggested to you;
  • for job applications: your role, city, portfolio or profile link and anything you tell us about yourself;
  • any message you write.

Only your name and phone number (or, for applications, your name, email, role and a link to your work) are required. Everything else is optional. Revenue answers are rough ranges, and you can choose "Prefer not to say".

Information collected automatically.

  • Visitor statistics. We use GoatCounter to count page views. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other websites and doesn't store your IP address. We see totals such as how many people visited, which pages, the referring site, and the browser, screen size and country.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may log your IP address and browser details when you load the site, for security and to keep the service running.
  • Fonts. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Browser storage. The site saves a few small notes in your own browser: the country you picked for prices, whether you closed the "free digital plan" prompt, and a timer that stops the same form being sent twice within a few seconds. They are not cookies, they never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

How we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry, prepare a proposal or quote, and follow up on it;
  • to tailor advice to your business, using your digital plan answers;
  • to consider your application to work with us;
  • to understand, in total, how people use the site so we can improve it;
  • to keep the site secure and prevent spam and abuse.

We don't use your information for automated decisions that affect you, and we won't add you to a mailing list without asking first.

Who else handles your information

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information. We use a few trusted services to run this website, and each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • FormSubmit passes form submissions to our email inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) stores the emails we receive.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts.
  • GoatCounter provides cookie-free visitor statistics.

If we start working together, we may use further tools (for example for project management, design or hosting your project), and we'll tell you which ones. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

International transfers

The services above may store or process data outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. By using our forms, you understand that your information may be transferred there, and we only use providers that take reasonable steps to protect it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and the conversations that follow for as long as we need them to work with you, and for up to 24 months after our last contact. Job applications are kept for up to 12 months in case a suitable role opens up. After that we delete them, unless we need to keep something longer for legal or accounting reasons. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

How we protect it

The site is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and has no user accounts or database of its own. We use a strict content security policy, limit which outside services the site can talk to, and have spam protection on our forms. Access to the inbox that receives your messages is limited to our team. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you promptly if a problem affects you.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • stop contacting you.

If you're in the EU, the UK or another place with data protection laws, you may have further rights, such as objecting to how we use your data or complaining to your local data protection authority. Where those laws apply, we rely on your request (to reply to you and prepare a quote) and our legitimate interest in running and protecting the site. To use any of these rights, email saad.bawanyy@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and professionals. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you think a child has sent us their details, tell us and we'll delete them.

Links to other sites

Our site links to other websites and apps, such as Google Play, LinkedIn and our products' own sites. We're not responsible for how they handle your information, so please read their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or the law changes. We'll change the date at the top when we do, and if a change is significant we'll make it clear on the website.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email saad.bawanyy@gmail.com.
