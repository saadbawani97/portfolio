FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Something else on your mind? Just ask.

How much does a website or app cost?

It depends on what you need. A simple business website costs far less than a full online store or a mobile app. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll send a clear quote before any work starts.

Can I hire you for just one thing?

Yes. Plenty of clients start with one thing, like a logo, a website or a month of social media, and add more later.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. We're based in Karachi and work with businesses across Pakistan and abroad. Most of the work happens over WhatsApp, email and video calls, so distance isn't a problem.

Can I see work you've done for clients?

Most client projects are confidential, so we don't publish them. What you can see are our own live products (QuickStop AI, Kaiora, Wayzos and Brainic) and the concepts in our studio lab. On a call we're happy to walk you through more.

What happens after launch?

Your choice. We can stay on to run your social media, ads, updates and automations, or hand everything over so your team can run it.
