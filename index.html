About

Engineer and delivery lead, at the same time.

I'm a Software Engineer who also leads delivery and builds products, currently Implementation Manager and Lead Frontend Engineer at 5StarDesigners.

I don't describe myself as purely a developer or purely a project manager, because in practice I've never been able to do one without the other. The requirements decide the architecture, and the architecture decides what's realistic to promise.

So I work the whole path: turning an idea into something a team can execute, building alongside them, testing it properly, and shipping it.

Alongside my full-time roles I've done freelance work, building and delivering web and mobile projects for clients. And I don't stop at the build: I've run marketing campaigns too, with Meta Ads, ASO and social media, to get products in front of people.

Wayzos

Carpool with people already on your route.

Peer-to-peer carpooling for Pakistan. It matches riders and drivers already heading the same way, even for part of a route. Cars and motorbikes, every member verified by a person before they can post or message.

Female-only matching is enforced on the server, not the screen, and chat works without anyone handing over a phone number.

Google Play wayzos.com
Case study
Problem
Carpooling only works if people trust who they ride with, and in Pakistan that has to hold for women riders too.
What I built
Route matching that pairs riders and drivers even for part of a route, person-verified members, female-only matching enforced on the server, and chat that never exposes a phone number.
Result
Live on Google Play and wayzos.com.

Process

Idea, ship, iterate.

  1. UnderstandThe problem, the users, the requirements.
  2. DefineClear features and user stories, not a wish list.
  3. PlanArchitecture, dependencies, resources, delivery.
  4. BuildWith the team, not over the top of it.
  5. TestFunctionality, UX, edge cases, real-world use.
  6. LaunchA usable MVP beats waiting for perfect.
  7. LearnFrom what people actually do with it.
  8. IterateAnd keep going.
